The motor model, its equations and the speed-control methods are those of
the companion paper, *Speed Control of Universal Motors in Sewing Machines*,
and its sources. The simulations and drawings are generated by
`tools/make_motor_waveforms.py` and `tools/make_motor_drawings.py` in the
project repository. The cordless-tool spectra in §5 are Robert Drzyzgula's
measurements, phone screenshots kept in `docs/images/motors/measured/`, read
back and replotted by `tools/make_tool_spectra.py`.
