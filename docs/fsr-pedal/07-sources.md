# Sources

1. Interlink Electronics, *FSR 400 Series Integration Guide*: construction,
   resistance against force, interface circuits, current limit, usage
   notes. Available from Interlink's web site.
2. Interlink Electronics, *FSR X & UX Series Integration Guide*: the same
   for the extended-range sensors, with their performance table (stand-off
   resistance, hysteresis, drift, repeatability) and the FAQ quoted in §2
   (drive voltage, resistance range, overload, lifetime).
3. [BTA16, BTB16 and T16 series 16 A triacs](https://www.st.com/en/thyristors-scr-and-ac-switches/bta16.html),
   STMicroelectronics data sheet (Snubberless BW version).
4. DB3, DB4 and DC34 diacs, STMicroelectronics data sheet (breakover 28–36 V, breakback at least 5 V).
5. 241KD14 metal-oxide varistor, Yageo 14D series data sheet.
6. General Electric, *SCR Manual*, 6th ed., 1979: phase control, RC timing
   networks and zener-clamped timing supplies.
7. *Inside a Switching Circuit*, the companion paper in this repository
   (`docs/switching/`): §3 on triac quadrants, §4 on phase control and
   snap-on, §5 on snubbers, §6 on the KB3151C.
8. *Speed Control of Universal Motors in Sewing Machines*, in this
   repository (`docs/motor-control/`): §4 on the carbon pile, §9 on the
   MCU controller.
9. IEC 60335-1, *Household and similar electrical appliances: Safety, Part
   1: General requirements*, for insulation classes, creepage and
   clearance.

The simulation and the figures are generated by
`tools/make_fsr_pedal_figures.py` in the project repository. The
schematic is drawn by the same script; there is no KiCad source yet.
