# Sources

1. I. Skalka, [*Low-Cost Universal Motor Phase Angle Drive System*](https://nxp.com/docs/en/application-note/AN1662.pdf), Freescale AN1662.
2. [BTA08, BTB08 and T8 series 8 A triacs](https://www.st.com/en/thyristors-scr-and-ac-switches/bta08.html), STMicroelectronics data sheet (Snubberless BW version).
3. [BT136 series triacs](https://pdf.elecfans.com/PHILIPS/BT136-600E.html), Philips Semiconductors product specification (BT136-600E).
4. [MOC3023M random-phase triac driver optocoupler](https://www.onsemi.com/pdf/datasheet/moc3023m-d.pdf), onsemi data sheet.
5. [Thyristor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thyristor), [TRIAC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TRIAC), [DIAC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DIAC), [Power MOSFET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_MOSFET) and [Insulated-gate bipolar transistor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insulated-gate_bipolar_transistor), Wikipedia, for the devices' histories.
6. General Electric, *SCR Manual*, 6th ed., 1979: the classic practitioner's handbook of thyristor circuits, including phase control and its RC networks.
7. N. Mohan, T. M. Undeland and W. P. Robbins, *Power Electronics: Converters, Applications, and Design*, 3rd ed., Wiley, 2003.
8. A. Hughes and B. Drury, *Electric Motors and Drives: Fundamentals, Types and Applications*, 5th ed., Newnes, 2019.

The device physics in §2, §3 and §7, the two-transistor model of the
thyristor included, is standard textbook material, given here without
citation line by line; Mohan, Undeland and Robbins is the standard
engineering text. The firing simulation and the figures are generated by
`tools/make_triac_figures.py` in the project repository. The KB3151C's
schematic was traced from photographs of the board; its KiCad source is in
`hardware/triac-board/`.
